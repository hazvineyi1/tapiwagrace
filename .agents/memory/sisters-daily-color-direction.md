name: Sisters Daily color direction
description: Color guidance for 31 Sisters Daily copy within the 31&Rooted site.
---

Use the original 31 Sisters Daily writing palette for that section: warm brown ink, terracotta accent, and softened warm-muted text. The 31 & Rooted wordmark and the Sisters Daily marks also use terracotta. Keep the wider site palette and meal photography unchanged unless specifically requested.

**Why:** The owner asked to restore the initial color scheme for 31 Sisters Daily while preserving the newer layout.

**How to apply:** Keep the full name “31 Sisters Daily” in the section writing and use the scoped Daily copy styles and terracotta brand marks instead of changing the site-wide palette.
