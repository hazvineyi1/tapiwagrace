---
name: Meal program positioning
description: Content guidance for presenting 31 Sisters Daily's meal support offering.
---

The meal offering should be described as a real, paid meal support and delivery program, with the cost structure made clear without inventing a per-meal dollar amount when none has been provided.

**Why:** The original section described a creative content service, which misrepresented the actual Meals on Wheels-style program.

**How to apply:** Use paid-program language, explain that pricing depends on meal count and delivery needs, and confirm the per-meal cost during enquiry until the owner provides a fixed rate.
